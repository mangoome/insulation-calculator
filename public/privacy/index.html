Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Overview

Insulation Calculator is designed to calculate insulation material requirements from work orders. We do not require an account and do not operate an application server for your calculations.

Information processed on your device

Images selected for OCR, extracted text, calculations, and saved calculation history are processed and stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to or stored by us. You can remove locally saved history at any time from the app.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites. You can manage or opt out of personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings. You can also learn more about how Google uses data when you use sites or apps that use its services at How Google uses data from sites and apps that use our services.
